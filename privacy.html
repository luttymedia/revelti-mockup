Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

1. Personal Data Collected and Purpose of Processing

We collect and process the following data for specific purposes:

  • Explicit Data: Email address and your selected user role (Organizer, Creative, or Attendee).
    Purpose: To send you recurring marketing newsletters, waitlist updates, and notifications regarding the launch of the Revelti application.
    Legal Basis: Your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) provided when submitting the form.
  • Technical & Server Data: IP addresses, User-Agent strings (browser/OS), and access timestamps.
    Purpose: Security, web server infrastructure monitoring, and protection against Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks.
    Legal Basis: Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) to maintain the security and integrity of the website.
  • Analytics Data: Pageviews, referral sources, clicks, and role selections.
    Purpose: To understand user interactions and improve the website.
    Legal Basis: Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in understanding the performance of our website.

2. Third-Party Services and Data Processors

We share your data with the following third-party service providers, acting as Data Processors:

  • Brevo: Used for managing the mailing list and sending newsletters. Data is stored within the European Union (France, Germany, Belgium).
  • PostHog: Used for website analytics in cookie-less mode. Data is stored on servers located in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Render: Used for website hosting and global Content Delivery Network (CDN) distribution.

3. International Data Transfers

Because Render operates a global CDN infrastructure, your technical data (IP addresses and access logs) may be routed or processed on servers outside the European Economic Area (EEA). These transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or other lawful mechanisms defined by the provider to comply with GDPR requirements.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

This website does not use cookies. We use localStorage strictly to save your functional UI language preference (revelti_lang). Because this does not track individual identities across the web and is strictly necessary for the user interface preference, a cookie consent banner is not required. Analytics are conducted via PostHog in a cookie-less configuration.

5. Data Retention

Your email address and selected role will be retained until you withdraw your consent (unsubscribe) or until the marketing purpose ceases to exist. Technical and analytics logs are retained only as long as necessary for security and performance purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Request the rectification of inaccurate data.
  • Request the deletion (erasure) of your data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (e.g., by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in our emails).
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your data.
  • Request data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, contact info@revelti.com. If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
